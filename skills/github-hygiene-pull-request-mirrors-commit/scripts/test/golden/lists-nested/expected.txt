Split the importer into a reader and a writer

What moves where:

- The reader parses the feed and validates each record against the schema, rejecting a record whose timestamp is in the future.
  - Rejected records go to the dead-letter queue, with the reason attached.
  - Accepted records go on.
- The writer batches records and commits them in one transaction.

  It retries a batch once on a serialisation failure, then gives up and reports the batch.

2. Then the two together.
